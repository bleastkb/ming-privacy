Ming — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 October 2026

The short version

Your birth date, birth time and birth place stay on your phone. Ming has no account, no analytics and no cloud sync. Only the Ask feature sends anything off the device, and what it sends is described below.

Stays on your phone

  • Birth date, birth time and birth place — yours and anyone you save.
  • Your chart, your daily reading and every relational reading. They are calculated on the device and work offline.
  • What you marked or wrote on the recognition screen. It is never sent anywhere.
  • Saved casts and settings.

Sent when you cast (Ask)

Pressing “Cast the lines” sends, through Ming’s reading proxy to DeepSeek (api.deepseek.com):

  • the question you typed and your chosen interests;
  • the hexagram;
  • a short summary derived from your chart: Day Master and its strength, the element in excess, the element lacking, the current ten-year period, and whether today favours you;
  • if you ask about someone you saved: their first name and the relation between the two charts.

This is used only to write that one reading. DeepSeek is a third-party service whose servers are in China. Raw birth dates, times, coordinates and full names are never sent. Your question is not linked to an account or identity, because Ming has none.

The proxy forwards your request and does not store it. Ming does not keep the content of your questions on any server. DeepSeek processes requests under its own terms and privacy policy.

Location search

Looking up a birth place uses Apple’s map search on your device and sends no personal information to Ming.

Purchases

Purchases are handled by Apple through the App Store. Ming never sees your payment details.

Calendar and notifications

If you choose, Ming adds the day’s action to your calendar and sends one local notification a day. Both are optional and processed only on your device.

Tracking and analytics

None. Ming does not track you, show ads, or include analytics or crash-reporting SDKs. If you join a TestFlight beta, Apple may share crash reports and any feedback you choose to send with the developer, under Apple’s terms.

Children

Ming is not directed at children under 13.

Your choices

Resetting the chart in Settings erases your data from the phone; deleting the app removes everything. Because Ming holds no account or server-side profile, there is nothing else to delete.

Changes

If what Ming sends ever changes, this page and the in-app “What leaves this phone” page change first.
